the brief

Innovate. Prototype. Transform Healthcare.

TECH4LIFE 2026 – Medical Devices Hackathon is a 36-hour innovation challenge that brings together students, innovators, developers, designers, and healthcare enthusiasts to solve real-world healthcare challenges through technology.

Participants will work in teams to ideate, design, build, and prototype innovative medical-device solutions, while getting guidance and mentorship from experts. Open to students from any discipline — no prior healthcare background required.

The Top 3 teams qualify for Phase 2 at IIT Bombay, with travel, accommodation, and food fully sponsored by IEEE. Bring your ideas, build something meaningful, and take your first step toward IIT Bombay.

the format

Two phases. One shot at IIT Bombay.

TECH4LIFE runs as a qualifier-to-final pipeline — build at ADYPU, and if you're in the top 3, build again on the national stage.

STAGE01
Qualifier · Ajeenkya D Y Patil University

Phase 01 — The ADYPU Sprint

A 36-hour on-site build at Ajeenkya D.Y. Patil University, Pune. Teams tackle a medical/healthcare problem statement end-to-end — from diagnosis to working demo — in front of judges from academia and industry.

↓  top 3 teams advance
STAGE02
Finals · IIT Bombay

Phase 02 — The IIT Bombay Finals

The top 3 teams from Phase 01 travel to IIT Bombay to compete at the national stage. Format, dates, and finals problem statements will be announced closer to Phase 01's conclusion.
